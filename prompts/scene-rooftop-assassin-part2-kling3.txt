Cinematic action scene. Ancient Middle Eastern street — weathered
stone walls, arched architecture, cloth banners shifting in the
wind, dust drifting through shafts of light, deep shadows across
the cobblestones.

The hero: dark wavy hair, blue eyes, long black trench coat. His
right arm from shoulder to hand is full cyberpunk mechanical metal
— plated, articulated, with red-accented joints. His left hand and
arm are completely normal human flesh. He carries two distinct
pistols — a tactical Glock-style pistol with teal-glowing circuits
in his normal left hand, and a 1911-style pistol with a wood grip
and teal-accented barrel in his full metal right hand.

An assassin in a white geometric hooded robe, brown leather armour
straps across the chest, red sash at the waist and dark gloves,
drops straight down from the rooftop above — white robes tearing
open in the fall, a slender double-edged blade locked out from a
leather bracer beneath his right sleeve, aimed point-down at the
hero's chest. The hero raises one pistol and fires a single shot
upward. A burst of deep crimson paint-like splatter explodes
outward in thick brushstrokes, white robes blown hard sideways,
head snapping sharply on impact. Visceral and immediate, fully
rendered in painterly stylized strokes. Body goes limp mid-fall
and drops heavily onto the stone at the hero's feet. Dust kicks
up around it.

The hero lowers the pistol and looks down at the body. His
expression is completely flat. Unimpressed. Unmoved.

Tight close-up on his face — eyes down, jaw still. He says,
low and dry:

"Noob."

Sound design: rushing air as the assassin falls, a single sharp
gunshot, the limp body hitting stone, dust settling, silence —
then the hero's one quiet line. No music.
Stylized 3D hand-painted animation, painterly brushstroke textures,
dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, high contrast deep shadows, rich
saturated colour palette, 4K cinematic quality, expensive production.
